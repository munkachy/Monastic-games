Monastic Games

Ora et labora et lude.

Benedictine Bricks Build the abbey tower as high as you can, and drive the demon off with holy water. Canticle Mansion A Benedictine monk explores a mansion made of the Canticle of Canticles: climb, stack, leap from walls, lead the goats home, and find every verse. Made for phones. Luminaries A falling-block music puzzle in the spirit of Lumines through all 38 Doctors of the Church: each Doctor a world of colour, a song and a gift of their own, with their words passing beneath the field. Easy, Normal and Hard, a tutorial, and Master mode. Made for phones. Psalter Runner A 16-bit runner through all 150 psalms with St. Therese, St. Joseph of Cupertino, St. Benedict, St. Maurus and St. Scholastica, and a saint to unlock for each book of the Psalter. Play a book, a random psalm or any psalm you pick, or watch the Blessed Virgin Mary run it perfectly. Made for phones: tap left to jump, right to attack. SaintStyle Turbo The saints race the seven deadly sins through the seven mansions of St. Teresa's Interior Castle. Fly off great ramps, ride the wind of the Spirit, and swipe tricks in the air: land them for style points and a surge of speed, the only way past a demon who tricks too. Funk music that builds as you speed up, and a Novitiate to teach you. Made for phones. Catholic Truth Squad: Tactics Assemble a squad. Level up. Light up the world. A team battler starring today's Catholic apologists. Still under review.

Fear Not A desert monk rides his guardian angel over a city of shadows. Leap the neon-noir rooftops, then drop down to fight the demons tempting a father in his car, in drunken-master kung fu: never the same blow twice. The first night, as a first build. Made for phones, played sideways.
