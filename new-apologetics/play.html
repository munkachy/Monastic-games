New Apologetics

A new era in Catholic apologetics has begun, and these are the apologists leading the way. Build your team, take on nine chapters of debates, and watch the story unfold between battles. Every debater starts at level 1; play them to discover what each one does best. Wear down an opponent's Composure or his Zeal and he's out of the debate. Your part is to clear the ground; the conversions are the Holy Spirit's work, so watch for them.
