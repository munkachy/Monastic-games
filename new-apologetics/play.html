New Apologetics

A new era in Catholic apologetics has begun, and these are the apologists leading the way. Build your team, take on five chapters of debates, and watch the story unfold between battles. Every debater starts at level 1; play them to discover what each one does best. Wear down an opponent's Composure or his Zeal and he's out of the debate. Conversions are rare, and they're worth watching for.
