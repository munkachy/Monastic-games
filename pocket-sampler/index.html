The export is the pattern (or the chain) as one seamless loop, ready for Remixlive.

Tone variation at 0 is a dead flat robot monotone. On sounds 1–8 the voice plays up the scale; on 9–16 it is chopped, one syllable per key. The voice engine is eSpeak NG from Vox Machina (about 18 MB the first time). Synthetic voice; no human voice recordings used.

Play: tap the keys. Sounds 1–8 are melodic (the keys go up the scale). Sounds 9–16 are kits and chops (each key is one hit).

Sound + key: choose a sound. Pattern + key: choose one of 16 patterns.

Write turns writing on and off. Stopped: the 16 keys are the 16 steps. Play a note first, then tap steps to place it; tap again to remove it. Playing: whatever you play is recorded into the beat, on the nearest step.

FX: hold a key to punch an effect in; let go and it's gone. Loops, rolls, scratch, half time, scramble, tape stop, octaves, reverse, unison, crush, low pass and echo. With Write on while playing, the effect is saved on every step you hold it through. "Clear effects" takes them out again.

Locks: with Write on while playing, moving a sound slider saves that setting on the step that is playing, for the current sound only, as the PO-33 does with its knobs.

● Rec: hold it and sing, speak, or beatbox into the phone. Let go and it lands on the current sound, chopped if it's 9–16. While holding Rec, tap a key to record into that sound instead. Set "Rec from" to the beat to resample what's playing.

Trim: Start and Length cut the sound down. On 9–16 they trim only the slice you played last.

🎲 Roll: a brand-new video-game sound on the last key you played. "Roll all 16" fills a whole kit.

On the screen, tap the waveform to hear that slice; drag a marker to move where it is cut.

Keyboard: 1234 / QWER / ASDF / ZXCV are the keys, space is play.

Video-game sounds: Juhani Junkala's "Essential Retro Video Game Sound Effects Collection" and Deva's "8-bit Sound Effect Pack", both CC0, from OpenGameArt. The kits, basses and rolled sounds are made in code.
