Pick a psalm and verse and its words go into the box, a line for each half-verse (each new line is a breath in the rap); or type your own. ⬆ adds your Grail file (the psalter pack Selah takes); it stays in this browser on this device and is never sent anywhere. Tone variation at 0 is a dead flat robot monotone. On sounds 1–8 the voice plays up the scale; on 9–16 it is chopped, one syllable per key. Rap it on the beat puts one syllable on each key of this sound and writes them into the pattern in the flow you choose, carrying on into the next patterns (chained) if they don't fit in one bar. Stressed syllables (shown in capitals) are put on the beat; a comma rests to the next beat and a full stop or new line to the next half bar (old school: the next bar). Change the flow, or tap 🎲 New flow, and the same rap is laid out again without speaking it again. Cut: Natural speaks the line as a whole and cuts it at the quiet dips; Robot-precise speaks each syllable on its own, so every cut is clean but it sounds more clipped. The voice engine is eSpeak NG from Vox Machina (about 18 MB the first time). Synthetic voice; no human voice recordings used.

The screen holds what you need while playing. ☰ Menu holds the rest: Sounds (load a sound onto the current key), Settings for that sound, Beat and tempo, Voice, your Buttons, Help and the Log.

    
The blue buttons, four beside Menu and eight under the keys, are yours to set (Menu › Buttons): Mute drums, Export, Roll, Beat, Clear, Key wheel, Next key, Oct − / Oct +, 🤖 Voice (opens the Voice page), 🎤 Rap (raps the words typed there onto the current sound), 🎲 Flow (a new flow for that rap), or any FX. The octave buttons move the keys you play on the current sound up or down an octave (two at most; the screen shows OCT+1), and notes you record keep the octave they were played in.

    
Chain (a blue button, or Menu › Beat): tap it, tap patterns in the order they should play, tap it again. Tap it twice without choosing any to unchain. Choosing a pattern while chained only shows it: the chain plays on.

    
Clear (a blue button) clears the pattern; with Sound on, it clears the chosen sound instead: off its key, and out of the patterns. ↶ Undo brings either back. ■ Stop also stops every loop that is running.

    
Loops (Menu › Sounds › Loops): drum loops, breakbeats, vinyl and ambience, one loop per key, in Loop mode. While the beat plays, a loop comes in at the next bar, in step, and follows the tempo and key; it stops at the next bar when tapped again, and every loop stops with the beat. Songs (Packs › Songs): songs written for the app from its own sounds, ready to play, chained.

    
Copy / Paste copy the pattern, or in Sound mode the sound: Sound + key 1, Copy, Sound + key 5, Paste.

    
Beats: pick a style in the Beat tab and put its beat into the current pattern, or press 🎲 Beat for a new one in that style, as often as you like. It plays on the drum kit you have chosen (or the first kit on the keys), and replaces only the drums already there: your bass and other sounds stay. ↶ Undo takes back any change, one at a time.

    
Pads and Loops (the two tabs at the top): Pads is the sampler; Loops is a grid like Remixlive's, always laid out sideways (held upright, it is turned so it reads right with the phone held sideways). Eight columns in their parts' colours (Kick, Snare, Tops, Bass, Chords, Lead, Sub, Vox/Keys), six cells each. Tap a cell and it starts on the next bar, in time and in key, and ▶ Play starts if it wasn't playing. One cell plays in a column: tap another and it takes over on the bar; tap the playing one and it stops on the bar; tap a waiting (blinking) one to take it back. The ring shows how far through its loop it is. Hold a cell (or use ✎ Edit) for its settings: Loop, One shot or Gate (only while held); its own volume; Transpose in half steps; Repeat 2, 4, 6, 8, 16 or 32 beats (a longer sound is cut there and repeats); Time stretch on (it keeps its pitch at any tempo) or off (it speeds up and slows down with the tempo, like a record); Follow the key (♪ on the cell; turned on, its key is found by listening); an Effect (low pass, high pass, reverb, echo, ping-pong, drive or tape, with an amount) that goes on the whole column, which any loop can be set to skip; its sound, from the loops and packs; and its wave: drag across it to choose the part that plays (it snaps to 16th notes on a loop with a tempo), tap it to hear from there, ▶ Hear it plays the part chosen. ⚙ holds the settings for all the loops: Quantize (when a tap lands: the next beat, bar, 2 bars or 4 bars, or Off: at once, still in time), Fade in and out, and Stop all loops (on the next point, the Pads beat playing on). Under each column: its volume (drag) and M to mute it. Tempo, key, Play and the effects are the same in both tabs, and ■ Stop stops everything. The grid starts with the app's own drum loops, breaks, vinyl and hype words; a set made for it comes next.

    
Colours go by part, the same everywhere, in the Pads and the Loops: a kick is always red, a snare orange, hi-hats yellow, a bass green. Sounds 1–8 are instruments, one part each: 1 Bass (green), 2 Sub (purple), 3 Chords (teal), 4 Lead (pink), 5 Vox/Keys (blue), 6 Pad (indigo), 7 Pluck/Arp (lavender), 8 Stab/FX (magenta). Each starts with a sound for its part (an 808 bass on 1, a Rhodes on 3…); load anything you like on any of them. Sounds 9–16 are drum kits and percussion. In Sound mode each pad shows its colour; in Play the keys take the current sound's.

    
Write: 🥁 Percussion / 🎹 Instruments (the two buttons over the keys while Write is on) choose what the steps show. Percussion: the drum hits, a block for each part hit on the step (kick red, snare orange, clap amber, hats yellow, open hat lime, toms coral, percussion mint, cymbals cyan). Instruments: a block for each instrument on the step, in its sound's colour. One fills the step, two to four split it side by side, five or more make a small grid. Choosing a sound switches to its view. The step with the current sound has a white light, and its block a glow; the step now playing has an orange ring. Menu › Buttons › Look brings back the classic beige look.

    
Play: tap the keys. Sounds 1–8 are melodic (the keys go up the scale). Sounds 9–16 are kits and chops (each key is one hit).

    
Sound + key: choose a sound. Pattern + key: choose one of 16 patterns.

    
Write: tap once for Write, twice for Replace, three times for off. In Replace, playing over a part replaces it: from your first note, the sound's old notes go as the playhead passes and yours go in; if you don't play, nothing is deleted, and it stops replacing once a whole pattern passes without a note. With either on, stopped: the 16 keys are the 16 steps. Play a note first, then tap steps to place it; tap again to remove it. Playing: whatever you play is recorded into the beat, on the nearest step; turn Quantize off in the Beat tab to keep it exactly where you played it.

    
FX: tap a key to turn an effect on, tap again to turn it off (or hold, if you switch that in Menu › Effects, where you also choose the effect on each key, from 26: loops, rolls, scratch, pitch, reverb, echo, ping-pong, chorus, filters, crush, overdrive, fuzz, wavefold and tape saturation). Loops, rolls, scratch, half time, scramble, pitch dive, octaves, reverse, chorus, crush, low pass, reverb and ping-pong (an echo bouncing between your ears). In FX mode the screen shows Tracks (tap to choose which sounds the FX keys hit) and the amount of the last effect you touched. With Write on while playing, the effect is saved on every step you hold it through. "Clear effects" takes them out again.

    
Locks: with Write on while playing, moving a sound slider saves that setting on the step that is playing, for the current sound only, as the PO-33 does with its knobs.

    
● Rec: hold it and sing, speak, or beatbox into the phone. Let go and it lands on the current sound, chopped if it's 9–16. While holding Rec, tap a key to record into that sound instead. Set "Rec from" to the beat to resample what's playing.

    
Play as (Sound tab): One shot plays the whole sound however short the tap; Gate plays only while your finger is down; Loop starts repeating the sound, or the slice, when you tap it and keeps going until you tap that key again (the key stays lit). Switch the sound to One shot or Gate and its loops keep going, so you can play over them; come back to Loop and tap a looping key to stop it. In the beat, Gate and Loop notes last as long as they sounded when you recorded them live, or one step if you tapped them in.

    
✂ Editor (Settings › Edit this sound, or after an import): select part of a sound, crop it or cut it out, place the cut points that split it across keys 9–16, then put it on any sound or save it to My Sounds. Instruments, kits and synths open as a recording of the last note played.

    
My Sounds (Menu › Sounds): your saved sounds, ⬆ Import (WAV, MP3, or a screen recording: record with the iPhone's Screen Recording, then pick the video, and its sound opens in the editor), and Make a pack from up to 16 of them. Any pack can be opened to load one of its sounds alone.

    
Key: tap the key on the screen (C MIN) for the key wheel, over the keys. Majors are round the outside, minors inside, in circle-of-fifths order; the shaded keys sit well with the one you're in. Tap any key, or the Next button in the middle (up a fifth, then across to the relative key, by turns); 🎲 picks one of the shaded ones. While the beat plays, the change waits for the top of the next bar (the key blinks until then). With Write on, the key is saved in the pattern, and that pattern always plays in it (the key box is shaded); with Write off, the key changes for now and patterns keep their saved keys. "Forget pattern key" takes it out.

    
Its key (Settings, for recordings and imports): say what key a sound is in, or tap 🔍 to have it found by listening, and it moves to the song's key (never more than six semitones). Left on "Plays as recorded", drums and other recordings stay as they are.

    
Trim: Start and Length cut the sound down. On 9–16 they trim only the slice you played last.

    
🎲 Roll: a brand-new video-game sound on the last key you played ("Roll all 16" in the Sound tab fills a whole kit). 🎲 Beat: a new drum beat in the style chosen in the Beat tab.

    
1 Shot / Gate / Loop (the button next to Rec) switches how the current sound's keys play; it is the same as "Play as" in the Sound tab.

    
On the screen, tap the waveform to hear that slice; drag a marker to move where it is cut.

    
Keyboard: 1234 / QWER / ASDF / ZXCV are the keys, space is play.
