The screen holds what you need while playing. ☰ Menu holds the rest: Sounds (load a sound onto the current key), Settings for that sound, Beat and tempo, Voice, your Buttons, Help and the Log.

    
The buttons are in rows, a colour for each kind of thing. At the top: Sound, Pattern, FX and ☰ Menu (what the keys do); green, ▶ Play and Write; grey, Copy, Paste, Undo, Redo. The rest are yours to set (Menu › Buttons), and each takes the colour of what it does. At first: red, Chain; purple, Oct −, Oct +, Key; amber, the sound (⟳ Loop, ✂ Edit, Clear, ⏺ Loop rec); pink, effects (reverb, echo, low pass, crush). The Pads are for instruments now: the drums, the robot and the rap live in the Loops (🥁 Design, 🤖 Robot words), where they work better.

    
Chain (a red button, or Menu › Song): tap it, tap patterns in the order they should play, tap it again. Tap it twice without choosing any to unchain. Choosing a pattern while chained only shows it: the chain plays on.

    
Clear (a blue button) clears the pattern; with Sound on, it clears the chosen sound instead: off its key, and out of the patterns. ↶ Undo brings either back. ■ Stop also stops every loop that is running.

    
Copy / Paste copy the pattern, or in Sound mode the sound: Sound + key 1, Copy, Sound + key 5, Paste.

    
Drums never change key. When the key changes, the bass and every other instrument move to it, but the drums (the kits, hand drums, a single drum hit on a key, drum loops, and the Kick, Snare and Tops columns of the Loops) play exactly as they are, in any key.

    
If the sound crackles on an older iPad: Menu › Buttons › Sound engine › Light. The app notices when the sound keeps breaking up and suggests it. Light gives the device more time to make each moment of sound (a little more delay between tap and sound), works at a lower rate inside, uses less memory, has a shorter reverb and plays fewer notes at once. Sounds only tried in Menu › Sounds are let go when the menu closes, so they don't fill the memory.

    
Sideways: the Pads lie sideways, like the Loops: the screen and buttons on the left, the keys on the right. Held upright, the whole app is turned a quarter turn, so it reads the right way up with the phone held sideways (turn the phone to the left); if the phone really turns, it simply lies flat. Menu › Buttons › Pads › Upright brings back the tall layout.

    
Loops + Pads (the third tab at the top, where the app opens): the loops on the left, four columns a page (◀ ▶ flips to the other four), the 16 pads on the right, held sideways. The pads play the loop you tapped last (a drum loop too; a white dot marks its cell), in its colour: its first note, played down and up the key across the pads (the 8th pad, marked •, is the note as played; a melody's first note is moved to the key's root first, a drum's is left as it is). FX gives the pads their own EQ and effect, as a loop cell has: the panel takes the pads' place, with the EQ (low, middle and high, ±12 dB, with its curve; Flat EQ to start again) and effects (low pass, high pass, reverb, echo, ping-pong, drive, distortion, tape), as many at once as you like, each with its own amount; ✓ Done brings the pads back. It goes on everything the pads play, and on what ⏺ Rec records from them; FX stays lit while any of it is on. Drive works like a Tube Screamer pedal: the bass passes clean and only the middle and highs are pushed into soft clipping, then softened, so it grows warm and gritty without going muddy. Distortion works like a ProCo RAT pedal, the classic distortion: much more gain (up to about 50 dB, the highs more than the bass), two diodes clipping it hard into a buzzing, nearly square wave, then a low pass to take off the fizz; none of the clean sound is kept. Low, it is a light grit; turned up, it is fully saturated and holds every note out, the quiet ends of the notes brought up too, and the top grows darker, as on the pedal. Tape works like a tape machine (after ChowTape's model): the highs are boosted going on and cut coming off, so loud highs squash first; a soft, slightly lopsided saturation adds warmth; the low end gets tape's little bump; the top rolls off more as you turn it up; and on the loops it wobbles a touch, as tape does. Both stay at about the same loudness as you turn them up, so you hear the colour, not just more volume. One shot / Loop / Gate (the button beside FX; tap it to switch): One shot plays the whole note however short the tap; Loop sets the note going round from a tap until you tap that pad again (a ring marks it); Gate plays only while the pad is held down. What you record keeps it, with how long each note was held. Choke (Loops › ⚙ › Pads choke): on, each note you play cuts off the one before, one voice, like a bass or a lead (what you record keeps it); off, they ring over each other. Oct − and Oct + move the pads down or up an octave (two at most; what you record keeps the octave you played). While the beat plays, each tap lands on the next point of the Quantize (1/16, 1/8 or 1/4; a tap just after one plays at once); Free plays at once. While ⏺ Rec records, every tap plays at once and is kept exactly as you played it, never quantized; you can put it on the beat afterwards, in the cell's 🎹 Notes. ✂ Crop to choose the bit yourself: the loop's wave takes the pads' place; drag across the bit you want (it is heard at once, as the 8th pad); drag either edge (the white lines with handles) to move just that side; tap the wave to hear from there; ▶ Hear for the bit chosen; Auto to go back to the first note found by itself; Select all for the whole loop; Select none to clear it and drag a new bit (the pads keep the last one until you do); − and + zoom out and in (×2 a tap, down to 20 ms across), keeping the spot you last touched in place, so you can set an edge exactly; zoomed in, ◀ ▶ move along the wave; ✓ Done for the pads. Each cell keeps its own bit. The pads keep the loop last tapped even when its cell is emptied (the screen says so), until another loop is tapped. The pads here only play: to write patterns or choose their sounds, go to Pads. ⏺ Rec (beside them) records what you play on the pads, as a looper does. It first asks two things, in place of the pads: the Length (1/2 bar, 1, 2, 4, 8, 16 or 32 bars; kept for next time) and Into: New, a new cell (below), or, when the pads are playing a loop you recorded on them, Layer (what you play is added to its notes; it keeps its length, and its notes play round as you go) or Replace (it is recorded again from nothing). While it records, the column volumes under the grid give way to a red bar, the loop being recorded: its beats (or bars), the notes as you play them (the lower the note, the lower it sits; a layered loop's notes in white) and a line going round. The loop goes round and round until you tap ■ Stop: what you played on earlier times round plays again each time, so you can build it up a part at a time. It counts in first (Loops › ⚙ › Count in: Off, 1 or 2 bars): four clicks a bar, from the next beat (at once if you tap on one), wherever the song is, with no waiting for a bar and everything silent but the clicks; the recording starts on the beat after the last click, and it all comes back then; what you play keeps its place in the bar, so the loop plays in step (if the beat was stopped, Play starts); tap ■ Stop when done (before it begins, it is called off). 1/16 (beside it) puts each note you play on its 16th (not while recording); tap it for Free, the timing exactly as played, and again to go back; hold it for 1/8 or 1/4 (the same setting as the Pads' Quantize). Hold a recorded cell and it opens on 🎹 Notes: the notes you played, along its bars, each named, in rows one under the other. Zoom: Bar (the least room: 16 bars a row, a line a bar; the first time), 1/4 (8 bars a row, a line a beat), 1/8 (4 bars), 1/16 (2 bars, a line every 16th). At each zoom a slot (a bar, a beat, an 8th, a 16th) with anything in it shows one block, filling it: the note's name when it holds one note, ×3 and so on when more. Tap a block and everything in it is deleted (zoom in to pick out single notes); drag it left or right and everything in it moves together, by 16ths. A block dragged past the end of its row goes into the next.  Length: 1/8, 1/4 or 1/2 of a bar, or 1, 2, 4, 8, 16 or 32 bars (a short one repeats in time, a 1/8 every two 16ths); made shorter, any notes past the new end are deleted (the screen says how many). ↶ Undo takes back the last change. You can't add notes there or change which note a note is. Each change makes the loop again, a moment after you let go.

    
Loops, Pads and Loops + Pads (one button at the top left, showing the screen you are on: tap it and the three drop down): ↶ ↷ in the Loops' top row undo and redo changes to the grid: a sound put in or emptied, Clear all, cells moved or pasted, a cell's settings, column volumes, mutes and effects, Follow the tempo, and what you make or record into a cell (a quick run of changes, a fader dragged, is one step). Pads is the sampler; Loops is a grid like Remixlive's, always laid out sideways (held upright, it is turned so it reads right with the phone held sideways). Eight columns in their parts' colours (Kick, Snare, Tops, Bass, Chords, Lead, Sub/Color, Vox/Keys), six cells each. Tap a cell and it starts on the next bar, in time and in key, and ▶ Play starts if it wasn't playing. One cell plays in a column: tap another and it takes over on the bar; tap the playing one and it stops on the bar; tap a waiting (blinking) one to take it back. The ring shows how far through its loop it is (a One shot or a Gate shows a straight line instead, filling as it plays). Hold a cell (or use ✎ Edit) for its settings. In ✎ Edit the grid turns shades of amber, so you can see you are editing, and you can drag a cell to another place: onto an empty cell it moves, onto a full one the two swap (in its own column a playing loop plays on; moved to another column it stops). 🗑 Clear all (there only while ✎ Edit is on) empties every cell and stops the loops, for a fresh start; the sounds stay in the library, and ↶ Undo clear brings the grid back until you leave ✎ Edit. (To keep a grid first: Loops › ⚙ › 💾 Save the grid as a set.) Pattern + key: choose one of 16 patterns.

    
Write: tap once for Write, twice for Replace, three times for off. In Replace, playing over a part replaces it: from your first note, the sound's old notes go as the playhead passes and yours go in; if you don't play, nothing is deleted, and it stops replacing once a whole pattern passes without a note. With either on, stopped: the 16 keys are the 16 steps. Play a note first, then tap steps to place it; tap again to remove it. Playing: whatever you play is recorded into the beat, on the nearest step; turn Quantize off in Menu › Settings to keep it exactly where you played it.

    
FX: tap a key to turn an effect on, tap again to turn it off (or hold, if you switch that in Menu › Effects, where you also choose the effect on each key, from 26: loops, rolls, scratch, pitch, reverb, echo, ping-pong, chorus, filters, crush, overdrive, fuzz, wavefold and tape saturation). Loops, rolls, scratch, half time, scramble, pitch dive, octaves, reverse, chorus, crush, low pass, reverb and ping-pong (an echo bouncing between your ears). In FX mode the screen shows Tracks (tap to choose which sounds the FX keys hit) and the amount of the last effect you touched. With Write on while playing, the effect is saved on every step you hold it through. "Clear effects" takes them out again.

    
Locks: with Write on while playing, moving a sound slider saves that setting on the step that is playing, for the current sound only, as the PO-33 does with its knobs.

    
Play as (Sound tab): One shot plays the whole sound however short the tap; Gate plays only while your finger is down; Loop starts repeating the sound, or the slice, when you tap it and keeps going until you tap that key again (the key stays lit). Switch the sound to One shot or Gate and its loops keep going, so you can play over them; come back to Loop and tap a looping key to stop it. In the beat, Gate and Loop notes last as long as they sounded when you recorded them live, or one step if you tapped them in.

    
✂ Editor (Settings › Edit this sound, or after an import): select part of a sound, crop it or cut it out, place the cut points that split it across keys 9–16, then put it on any sound or save it to My Sounds. Instruments, kits and synths open as a recording of the last note played.

    
⏺ Loop rec (an amber button, ⏺ Loop): record what you play over the beat or the Loops, straight into a loop. Choose how: Notes (your taps on the pads, on the beat as Quantize puts them; afterwards ▶ Hear it, ✓ Keep it, or ✎ Fix it as patterns: the notes go into empty patterns, chained, to fix a note before ⟳ Make a loop) or Sound (the sound itself, like a looper pedal: the pads alone, without the Loops grid or the effects on the whole mix, or everything you hear); how many bars (1, 2, 4 or 8); and the Loops column it goes in (or a guess from what you play). Start: it begins on the next bar (if nothing is playing, Play starts and a bar counts in), shows the bar it is on, and stops by itself; tap it again to stop early. Kept, it goes in the first empty cell of its column (the next column with room, if that one is full), playing from the next bar, and in the library (My Sounds, its folder, My loops), in time at any tempo.

    
Design (now in the Loops: 🥁 Design a hit and 🎹 Design a sound, in a cell's picker): make your own sounds. 🥁 Drum hit: a kick, snare, clap, hat, open hat or ride, tom, percussion (wood, metal or glass: a woodblock, rimshot, cowbell, chime) or shaker, each a model of how that drum makes its sound, with its own knobs (a kick's pitch, how far and fast it drops, its length, click, beater, knock, sub; a snare's tuning, body and wires; drive, tone and Old sampler, a 1980s sampler's grit, on most). Start from one of its starting points, turn the knobs, ▶ Hear it; 🎲 Surprise makes a new one near a starting point, ↻ New strike the same with different noise. Each pitch shows its note (52 Hz A♭1), and ♪ In key tunes the drum to the nearest note of the song's key. Finish, for every drum: Soften the hit (less click), Low cut and High cut (so a layer keeps to its own range), Layer (a second drum under it, from any starting point, lined up so they never flam, at Layer level), Room (a short real room, baked in) and Reverse. ＋ Add puts it in a kit of yours (up to 16 hits; New kit makes one), which loads on a drum sound (9–16) from Menu › Sounds › My packs; ★ Save keeps it alone in My Sounds. 🎹 Synth: two waves (sine, triangle, saw, square; the second tuned and detuned), a sub an octave down, noise, unison voices; a filter (low, band or high pass) with resonance and an envelope that opens it; the volume's attack, decay, sustain and release; vibrato, a glide into each note, and drive. Start from Warm pad, Analog bass, Pluck, Supersaw lead, Howl, Bell keys or Organ, in a Bass, Middle or High range. ▶ Hear it plays one note, tapped then held; Put on sound records the whole instrument (a note every 6 half steps, tapped and held, a seamless loop for held notes) onto that sound, to play and record into patterns and loops; ★ Save keeps it in My Sounds. Every sound made here is made once and kept as a recording.

    
    
    
🎹 Design a sound (in the Loops: hold a cell, and choose it at the top of the picker, or the cell's 🎹 Sound tab): make a bass, keys, a lead or a pad of your own, for that cell. It starts from a sound that suits the column (Analog bass in Bass, Bell keys in Chords, Supersaw lead in Lead…); choose another under Start from, set its Range, turn the knobs (Basic shows the few that matter most: wave, sub, cutoff, resonance, attack, release, vibrato, drive; Advanced, all of them), ▶ Hear it, or 🎲 Surprise. 🎹 Play it on the pads records the whole instrument once (a note every 6 half steps, as every sound made in code is kept) and opens Loops + Pads with the pads playing it and the cell marked in red; play, and ⏺ Rec puts what you play in that cell (replacing what it held). Afterwards, tapping that cell puts the same instrument back on the pads, so you can play along with it or record more. ★ Save to My Sounds keeps the instrument.

    
🥁 Design in the Loops: hold a cell for its settings, and its third tab, 🥁 Design, makes a hit and puts it straight in: tap the steps of one bar (on, soft, off; drag a lit step along its line to move it; the column suggests a start: kicks on 1 and 3, snares on 2 and 4, hats in eighths), make the hit below (the same designer as Menu › Design), ＋ Add a line for another drum on its own steps (up to 6 lines: kick, snare, hat, clap and so on; tap a line's name to shape that one's drum, ✕ takes it away), ▶ Hear the bar, and ✓ Put in this cell: a one-bar loop at the song's tempo and swing, playing from the next bar, also kept in the library (My loops). An empty cell has 🥁 Design a hit at the top of its picker.

    
🎹 From the pads (at the top of a cell's picker: an empty cell, or Change the sound): opens Loops + Pads with that cell ready to record into (a red edge). Tap a loop for the pads' sound, then ⏺ Rec.

    
Basic and Advanced (at the top of Menu › Design and of a cell's 🥁 Design): Basic shows the few knobs that shape each sound most (a kick's pitch, pitch drop, length, click and drive; a snare's tune, body, wires and snap; a synth's wave, sub, cutoff, resonance, attack, release, vibrato and drive) and the Room; Advanced shows every knob, the layer, and the whole finish (soften, low and high cut, reverse). The choice holds for every designer on this device, and what you set in Advanced stays when you go back to Basic.

    
Sets and pack files (Loops › ⚙): 💾 Save the grid as a set keeps the whole grid (every cell and its settings, the column effects, tempo, key and reverb) in My sets, to load again in one tap (hold one to share, rename or delete it). ⬆ Share as a pack file makes one file with the grid and every sound of yours in it, to keep in Files or iCloud Drive; ⬇ Open a pack file (or My Sounds › ⬆ Import) opens one on any device: its sounds go into My Sounds and its set into My sets. My Sounds › ⬆ Share as a pack file puts all your sounds in one file (a backup, too).

    
☁ Send to the library (Menu › Sounds › My Sounds): sends a pack (the Loops grid, all your sounds, one sound, or a pack file) with notes on it (what it is; who made it: you, a recording you made of something else, or someone else, with where it is from and its license) to the repository, so it is kept for good and shows on every device in Menu › Sounds › ☁ Shared at once (tap one to add it to My Sounds). It needs a GitHub token, once on each device: a fine-grained token for munkachy/Monastic-games only, with Contents: Read and write; it is kept only in that browser (Forget my token clears it). Tell Claude, and what you sent is filed in the library and in the list of every sound and its license.

    
⟳ Make a loop (an amber button, ⟳ Loop): the pattern, or the chain of patterns as one long loop, made into a loop for the library. Name it, choose the folder it goes in (Beats, Bass, Chords, Lead, Sub, Vox and keys, Pads…; it guesses from what plays) and what plays in it (everything, everything but the drums, or only the drums); ▶ Hear it, then Save. It is then in that folder of Menu › Sounds (your loops first), in My Sounds, and in the Loops cells' sounds (My loops), and keeps time at any tempo. It is kept on this device.

    
🗑 Review the library (Menu › Sounds, at the top): every built-in sound, folder by folder, with ▶ to hear it and a Delete box. Packs and kits open (▸) so single sounds in them can be ticked. Ticked sounds leave the menus on this device at once; Send to Claude opens a GitHub issue listing them (tap Submit), or Copy the list to paste in a chat, and Claude deletes them from the app for good, so they are gone wherever Pocket Sampler is opened.

    
Menu › Sounds is in folders by part, in the parts' colours: Beats (red: whole beats: Human Grooves played by drummers, drum loops, breakbeats; and the Electro Boom Bap kick loops), Snares and claps (orange: every one of the kits', one by one), Tops (yellow: hats, cymbals, percussion, one by one), Drum kits, Bass (green), Chords (teal), Lead (pink), Sub and color (purple: subs, and the odd, glitchy accents: the video-game sounds, glitch and machines), Vox and keys (blue), Pads, Plucks, mallets and bells, Stabs and FX, ambience, songs and yours.

    
Favorites and My Sounds (Menu › Sounds): choose any sound, then ☆ Favorite (under Load) keeps it in ★ Favorites, and ★ Save to My Sounds keeps a copy of your own. My Sounds: your saved sounds, ⬆ Import (WAV, MP3, or a screen recording: record with the iPhone's Screen Recording, then pick the video, and its sound opens in the editor), and Make a pack from up to 16 of them. Any pack can be opened to load one of its sounds alone.

    
Key: tap the key on the screen (C MIN) for the key wheel, over the keys. Majors are round the outside, minors inside, in circle-of-fifths order; the shaded keys sit well with the one you're in. Tap any key, or the Next button in the middle (up a fifth, then across to the relative key, by turns); 🎲 picks one of the shaded ones. While the beat plays, the change waits for the top of the next bar (the key blinks until then). With Write on, the key is saved in the pattern, and that pattern always plays in it (the key box is shaded); with Write off, the key changes for now and patterns keep their saved keys. "Forget pattern key" takes it out.

    
Its key (Settings, for recordings and imports): say what key a sound is in, or tap 🔍 to have it found by listening, and it moves to the song's key (never more than six semitones). Left on "Plays as recorded", drums and other recordings stay as they are.

    
Trim: Start and Length cut the sound down (on a sound with slices, only the slice you played last).

    
On the screen, tap the waveform to hear that slice; drag a marker to move where it is cut.

    
Keyboard: 1234 / QWER / ASDF / ZXCV are the keys, space is play. In Loops + Pads the same keys play its pads (and ⏺ Rec records them); on the Loops alone they play nothing.
