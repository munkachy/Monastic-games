To make a WAV with an iPhone voice

1. In the Shortcuts app, make a shortcut with Make Spoken Audio from Text (choose the voice in the action), then Save File.
2. Choose that file below. Vox Machina runs it through the effects, Vowel length included, and ⬇ WAV exports it as usual.

Tempo & rhythm

Tempo sets the singing speed and keeps Wobble and Gate on the beat. Match your Remixlive BPM.

Voice

When singing: Pitch moves the tune up or down (±12 semitones), Pitch range widens or narrows its leaps (0 = one note), Speed sets how quickly words are said inside their notes, and Word gap separates the words. Vowel length (speech) stretches only the vowels when speaking, up to double; when singing, use Vowel length in the Melody panel.
