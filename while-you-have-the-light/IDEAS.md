# Ideas from watching Spelunky (kept for later; nothing built)

Strong, cheap:
- Cobwebs: slow him; spiders sometimes drop out. Generic in every mountain.
- Breakable clay jars/pots: merit, holy water, a heart, sometimes a spider.
- Skeletons rising from the ground (every mountain); leave a skull that can be thrown (memento mori). User to make art.
- Hidden spike / dart traps; a thrown block, skull or pot sets them off.
- Look down: hold down and the view lowers a little (for drops).
- Rest rhythm: about four lay-brother rests per mountain. A shrine candle (checkpoint) heals 1 heart; the lay brother heals all (with the hearts system).

Strong, bigger:
- Merit instead of money: from freeing worshippers, breaking idols, rescues. Lost on death unless banked at the lay brother.
- The hermit / elder: a rare hidden cell in a level. You give alms (merit) for the poor and he gives a blessing you choose among 2-3. Never "buying" a blessing (simony): alms freely given, a blessing freely given.
- Rescue a lost pilgrim: lead him to the lay brother for an extra heart.
- Saint companions from relics: a faint glowing figure that floats after him and helps by the saint's charism. St Benedict: just accompanies (his ward already protects).
- The dog: St Roch's dog, or St Dominic's dog with a torch in its mouth (Domini canes). Runs at a demon when sent and comes back. Carry it to protect it.
- Possessed people: like worshippers but with glowing eyes, wandering. A saint companion can intercede and free one; the saint then returns to heaven (you lose the companion, freely given). No altar, no sacrifice. (Mark 5, the Gerasene.)
- Or bring a companion home to the monastery for building perks by the saint.
- Avarice: the golden calf / gold brick: take it and a boulder comes rolling; it is heavy behind you like a block. Greed punished.

Later / maybe:
- Digging: only cracked walls can be dug (shovel or pick), opening secret side rooms. Not free digging everywhere.
- Water puts out the torch; relight at shrine candles.
- Throwing the torch into dark places (an unlocked ability).
- Skip: piranha.

New art needed: cobwebs and spider; clay jars; skeleton (rise, walk, crumble) and skull; spike and dart traps; the hermit in his cell; the dog; saint companions; possessed people (worshippers with glowing eyes); golden calf and boulder.
