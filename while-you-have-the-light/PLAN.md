Looking about was built and taken out (in the dark it shows little). Later: a small picture of the move in each tip.
2. Done (15 Oct 2026): the monastery in peace (no raids, no walls; the brothers walk about; day and night go on); hold on the ground to choose where the library and the choir stand; relics' powers last half their time, and 5% more for each brother received after the first three.
3. Hearts, herbs and the necrology (~95,000), settled 15 Oct 2026:
   - 3 hearts (no more to be had for now). A blow costs a heart and dims the torch by a third. The torch going out does not kill him: he goes dark (as into mortal sin: there is still time to repent), and must find light. A candle beside each carved verse relights and refills the torch, once each. Hearts come back only at a lay brother (with a cart): all of them, and the torch refilled, once for each brother. The third heart lost: he dies.
   - The blessing on keys: both mouse buttons at once (as two fingers on the screen). The blessing's own light is about a fifth of a full torch's (a little to see by in the dark).
   - Four herbs, gathered on every mountain: rue, hyssop, sage, wormwood. Two ground together make a potion; which pair makes which is fixed for each new game. Four effects, each about 10 s: float (he rises slowly by himself; down nudges him down), unseen (translucent; demons neither see nor strike him), slow fall (a third of his falling speed), a brighter torch. A potion does nothing one time in three; a little mortar and pestle, won from a summit's boss, makes it one in five. The hermit (chunk 13) takes any potion: drink it or trade it. Avarice's boxes take only gold.
   - Each life is one of the brothers: the first climber is one of the three free brothers at the start. On death his name is told, and he is written in the necrology (a book in the library, the latest first; kept from the start, read once the library stands). Everything not banked is lost; only blocks can be banked. No brothers left: the game is over, and everything is reset but the necrology.
   - Of those freed from the idols, about three in ten (at random) come down to the monastery gate; receiving one as a brother costs a block. So blocks go to building or to brothers.
4. Room grid + Gluttony's first climb (~170,000), Spelunky-style: the mountain is a grid of rooms (about 3 wide, 6-10 tall); a guaranteed path wanders UP from the entrance to the top, each path room marked by the openings it needs; rooms off the path are side rooms. Each room is a hand-made template (small grid maps) with 'maybe' tiles and random obstacle patches, so the same template comes out different each time. A second pass places creatures, jars, cobwebs and herbs by simple rules per spot. Special rooms (hermit's cell, lost pilgrim, shrine, herb cache) replace side rooms by chance, often sealed behind cracked walls. Hard climbing stays off the path; check-and-repair proves the path climbable. Fixed set pieces (the mouth at the bottom, boss rooms at the top) stay hand-made. Per-mountain settings; demon counting. Templates: zigzag shaft, pit gap (2-3 versions), corridor, elbow, low crawl, shrine room; wall dressing; trace the Gluttony backdrop pieces (art already filed); white-room piece picker.

Later: tower defense with the choir; side branches to hidden rooms (path-first method).
Rule: a thrown-down idol rebuilds itself only when demons carry its block back to it, or when its block is destroyed (in lava); then it can be broken again for a fresh block.
Rule: one-handed vs two-handed. Light things (fireball, fat glob, frog, skull, herb) are held in ONE hand: the torch arm stays out, torch and flare still work (new one-handed hold pose; today the torch arm folds in and the torch is hidden). Heavy things (blocks) take both hands: he sets the torch down (its light stays there), no flare until he takes it up again or reaches a shrine candle.
Rule: many creatures can be stunned, picked up and thrown (little frogs, the red demon, the skull).
Rule for every grab (frog, demon, octopus): while held or swallowed he cannot move left or right, only punch free.
Later: water. Wading or swimming puts out the torch; he goes dark but the blessing still gives a faint light, and with hearts the dark no longer kills. Relight at shrine candles. Short underwater tunnel pieces; swimming poses for the jointed monk in code; sea creatures (an octopus that wraps around him, others) need art.
Later: the cuculla, the flowing choir garment worn over the habit (drawn in code), as an item: hold the button while falling to glide down slowly.
Later: Wrath. A block that falls into lava is gone, and its idol rebuilds itself, to be broken again. Magma held over doors that open a second after you take the treasure beneath, then pours out; fire spouting from a pot, knocked over to aim it sideways at demons; boss: a demon with huge fists that leaps, hovers over him and slams down, invincible, but each slam breaks the floor until he smashes through into the lava below.
Later: a demon that turns into a floating spirit to follow him through walls.
