# Art notes

- boss-maw.png: the great demon of gluttony. Mouth half open; cut the jaw at the teeth.
- food-sheet-1.png: food platforms. Do NOT use the stack of cookies (middle right).
- demons-gluttony-1.png: ice-cream cone (bat wings), three-tier cake, wine-skin, wind-up teeth, sweets-imp.
- larder-1.png: 3 hams on hooks, 2 sausage strings, netted cheese, chain hook, 3 barrels (middle one has eyes: demon hides), 4 sacks, shelf of jars.
- banquet-1.png: 2 chandeliers on chains, roast pig platter, 4 goblets, jug, long table with fleur-de-lis runner, high chair, candelabra.
- stomach-1.png: round stomach chamber, 4 ledges (2 each side), drips from ceiling. Top and bottom tubes drawn solid: cut openings through them in code.
- idols-1.png: Ashtoreth (lust), golden calf (avarice), Moloch with furnace belly (wrath; furnace can glow).
- worshippers-1.png: 8 robed hooded figures (kneel arms up, bowed, stand arm up, swaying, prostrate, kneel praying, arms out, crouching). They wear monk-like cord belts: trim the cord so they don't read as monks.
- monastery-folk-1.png: hooded monks (walking, reading, bell) and tonsured lay brothers (sack, hoe, mason with bricks, leading a cow, basket). Hood = choir monk, tonsure = lay brother.
- bowels-1.png: coiled tube, knotted turn, ceiling folds, ribbed tunnel mouth, crawl opening. Tunnel mouths are drawn front-on (3D); use as decor at crawlway ends, not as walkable shapes.
- bowels-2-sections.png: side cross-sections: straight crawl tunnel, tunnel ending in flared opening, L-bend up, floor drop-hole, wall exit onto ledge. White inside = crawl space. These are the playable shapes.
